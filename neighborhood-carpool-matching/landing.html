FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know.

Common questions about joining, pricing, and scheduling on the Waymate network.

How are shared ride contributions calculated? +
Waymate calculates fixed fares based solely on splitting estimated fuel costs for the route distance across passenger seats. There are zero peak-hour multipliers, surge algorithms, or hidden booking fees.
How does member verification work? +
Commuters verify their account using institutional email addresses (university or corporate) along with phone authentication. Drivers must additionally supply their vehicle registration and valid driving license.
Can I book or offer rides for regular morning commutes? +
Yes. Most Waymate journeys are recurring daily commutes between residential sectors (such as Model Town and Urban Estate) and major hubs like Lovely Professional University, railway stations, and commercial complexes.
What if my schedule changes or I need to cancel? +
You can cancel pending requests or scheduled rides anytime directly from the "My requests" dashboard. We ask members to provide at least 30 minutes advance notice so matched neighbors can adapt their plans.
